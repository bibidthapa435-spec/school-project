Welcome to Jaljala Secondary School

Empowering Young Minds from Nursery to Class 10 (SEE)

Established in 2045 B.S. (1988 A.D.), Shree Jaljala Secondary School is a leading public educational institution located in Panchkhapan Municipality-7, Bihibare, Sankhuwasabha, Nepal.

Our school provides holistic basic and secondary education (Classes Nursery to 10), combining academic excellence, practical science laboratories, computer digital literacy, and traditional cultural values. We aim to prepare students to succeed in the Secondary Education Examination (SEE) and beyond.
