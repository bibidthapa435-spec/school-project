Campus Facilities

Infrastructure & Facilities

A well-supported learning environment with modern labs, safe spaces, and enriching student amenities.

Computer Workstation Lab

Digital Literacy

Equipped with desktop computers and basic digital learning resources. Students from Class 4 to Class 10 receive hands-on computer training in typing, document editing, spreadsheets, and safe internet browsing.

Practical Science Laboratory

Physics, Chem & Biology

Features microscopes, chemical apparatus, physics test instruments, and biological specimen models to help Class 9 and 10 students conduct practical experiments for SEE board preparations.

School Library & Reading Room

Books & Reference

Houses over 2,500 books including Nepalese literature, reference encyclopedias, SEE question banks, storybooks for primary children, and daily newspapers.

Playground & Sports Grounds

Football, Volleyball & Track

Spacious playground facilities for volleyball court, football practice, athletics sprints, badminton, and physical fitness assemblies under qualified sports instructors.

Primary Midday Meal Program

Government Subsidized

Freshly prepared hygienic midday meals (Diba Khaja) served daily to students in Class Nursery to Class 5 to support childhood nutrition and active learning.

Clean Drinking Water & Sanitation

Hygienic Restrooms

Filtered drinking water stations across campus and clean, separate gender-wise restrooms with first-aid emergency medical care kit.
