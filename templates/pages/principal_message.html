"Educating Hearts and Minds for a Brighter Tomorrow"

Greetings to all parents, guardians, students, and community members.

Welcome to Shree Jaljala Secondary School! It gives me immense pride and joy to welcome you to our official school portal. Since our inception in 2045 B.S. (1988 A.D.), our constant endeavor has been to create an educational ecosystem where academic rigor coexists with moral integrity, creative thinking, and sportsmanship.

At Jaljala, we cater specifically to students from Class Nursery up to Class 10 (SEE level). Our curriculum adheres strictly to the National Curriculum Framework of Nepal while integrating modern learning tools like computer lab practicals, science experiment demonstrations, and co-curricular house competitions.

We strongly believe that every child possesses unique talents. Our team of qualified and passionate teachers is committed to recognizing and nurturing those talents in a safe, disciplined, and supportive atmosphere.

I extend my heartfelt gratitude to the management committee, parents, municipal authorities, and alumni for their unwavering trust and support. Together, we shall continue to elevate the standards of education in Panchkhapan Municipality.

Warm Regards,
Principal,
Shree Jaljala Secondary School
