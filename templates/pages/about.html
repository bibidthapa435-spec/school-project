About Shree Jaljala Secondary School

History, Mission, Vision, and Educational Core Values

Established 2045 B.S. (1988 A.D.)

A Legacy of Educational Service in Panchkhapan

Shree Jaljala Secondary School was founded in 2045 B.S. with the vision of providing accessible, affordable, and high-quality education to children in Panchkhapan Municipality-7, Bihibare, Sankhuwasabha district.

Over 38 years, our institution has grown from a local primary school into a comprehensive secondary school offering education from Nursery to Class 10 (SEE level). Our alumni have gone on to pursue successful careers in medicine, engineering, public service, teaching, and entrepreneurship.

Our Mission

To nurture curious, disciplined, and morally sound individuals by providing engaging classroom instruction, practical science education, computer literacy, and inclusive co-curricular activities.

Our Vision

To be a benchmark public secondary school in Sankhuwasabha district, empowering every student with knowledge, technology, and leadership skills required to excel in national SEE examinations and life.

Core Objectives

100% academic SEE success, practical scientific experimentation, digital computer training, sportsmanship, environmental awareness, and preservation of Nepalese cultural traditions.

Key School Achievements

Consistently High SEE Pass Rate

Outperforming regional averages with top GPA scores in Science and Mathematics in Sankhuwasabha district.

Modern Computer Workstation Lab

Equipped with desktop computers providing basic digital literacy and keyboard skills for Class 4 to 10.

Annual Inter-House Sports Champions

Active participation in municipal football, volleyball, athletics, and traditional martial arts competitions.

Rich Cultural & Religious Traditions

Grand annual Saraswati Puja celebrations fostering community harmony and artistic talents among students.
